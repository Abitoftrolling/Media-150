Reading and Written Response #1

“Graphic Novel: Motherhood in crisis” by Laurence Ivil, Alicia Prager and Saidu Bah reflection

Before I start the actual review, I just wanted to make note of the comments before the actual Novels. Although this article was made in 2020, ie.during the start/peak of covid and although the information may be outdated, I at least know that the data is still somewhat true. I think I will talk about only 1 of these stories due to word count, although the overall scope of each story is drastically different from each other.

The first story we go through is called Memunatu: The distant patient ‘Birth in my village’. The first thing I notice upon the second(or first page) are the words “The majority of Women and girls give birth at home”. When the text first mentioned this, I got very scared of the implication of girl; whether it was talking about 18-21 year olds or people much younger than that. With the pictures shown, I believe it's more of the former rather than the latter. The graphics itself are quite akin to what you can see in other comics; it uses a lot of shading and simple shapes to portray location. There is also an extensive use of lines especially in page 7 as the top of the hut was covered with black lines to show the material it's made out of: hay. The story of Memunatu is a story of resilience as post her failed child birth, she feels ashamed to even talk to the other women of the village. This is until she realizes that the others are just like her. She then goes on to get an operation on her fistula. Then my favorite part of the story pops up. She mentions that she is still getting leaky so they give her a plug. The page directly after is then a IRL(In real life) picture of the plug along with her explaining how the plug works. This is ofc not the first set of IRL pictures used for this story, however, the first time i saw this it reminded somewhat of the comic “American born Chinese” by Gene Luen Yang, which used the same technique during parts of his graphic novel. The IRL pictures are used more heavily during the last parts of the novel including pictures of her at the clinic,pictures of her newborn, along with even a video of the doctor recounting her experience with fixing fistulas’ in women. This would only be possible, of course, on a computer setting rather than it being a physical copy. This is the charm of graphic novels specifically meant for the web as you can add a lot more detail to a Novel with videos, timed transitions of pages, etc. The ending however holds a dark omen as it tells us the start of the covid-19 pandemic was not too far behind the success story of Memunatu and sadly the clinic she went to be reborn closed shortly after.
