Creative statement:

I invite the viewer to see work created by the common man. To see work that, rather than invoke new feelings into a person, instead forces them to reconsider past feelings that they otherwise regarded as “useless” or “junk”. I try (and notability sometimes fail) to use simplicity and mundaneness in my works, but even in my failures, I see the true art of my work blossom. I'm not perfect, nor do I feel like it. I don't feel human and yet I hope my works can inhibit human feelings that i myself struggle to convey in daily life...other than laughing at my faults.
