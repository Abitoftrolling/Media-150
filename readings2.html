Reading and Written Response #2

With ‘AI slop’ distorting our reality, the world is sleepwalking into disaster by Nesrine Malik reflection

When I think of the current world of AI, I see a group of children grossly misunderstood due to its twin brother. When people speak on AI, they always speak on it in the general sense rather than directing that anger and hatred towards the real problem in today’s society, Generative AI. In the article created by Nesrine Malik, it now feels like somebody understands why this particular part of Artificial intelligence is creating a downward slope in our society. There is a really crucial part of her essay that undoubtedly caught my attention in regards to picture generation. She mentions different events that have happened currently within the years of 2025-early 2026(which is right now). The violence and the turmoil people have to go through in Gaza, the fear that is put on the face of immigrants in the face of ICE officers; pictures like these are then dropped in between the cesspool of AI generated images. Of course for those with the eyes to see it, it's quite simple to point out the wheat from the chaff, but for others the constant threat of an ai image forces people not to trust their eyesight. Something that was to be trusted above even sight and smell, now irrelevant in the wake of this mess of “ai slop”. As she says in her essay, pictures that you see entirely become “too real” and likewise “entirely unreal”. What do you trust anymore? Do you trust your instinct and not believe the truth that is set in front of you or do you keep pace with the lie that you keep telling yourself will become true. eventually.

Even with the cons of Artificial intelligence generating images for people, the common man/woman just can’t seem to stop. It's almost as if it has become a sort of comfort tool that allows you not to focus on the now. She does give this example in her own essay, but I'd like to give a separate example in a similar vein. On X(formerly known as twitter), The rise of the use of the AI chatbot, “Grok”, had become prevalent not only in its use for fact checking X users, but also its use of AI manipulation of images people post on the website. A recent example of this I have found was on the gold medalist Alysa Liu. After her win in the figure skating singles, some X users tried to use her for their own agenda against other communities and used AI manipulated photos of her to paint her in a light that wasn’t her own. This, of course, was without her knowledge and without her consent as well…but would the community rallying around that picture know that? For most not due to the fact that rather than doing the research needed to confirm such a picture, they instead agree with it because it agrees with their own ideals. Why allow the truth to flourish when the lie fits your perfect agenda. Why need to concern yourself with the truth when the lie is a much sweeter feeling.
